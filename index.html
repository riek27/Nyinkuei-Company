Who We Are

Building Stronger Communities Through Peace and Development

PEACE AND DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION (PDO) is a National Non-Governmental Organization (NNGO) and non-profit making organization founded in South Sudan since 2020 and legally registered in 2021 with the Relief & Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) under Chapter 3, Section 10 of the NGOs Act, 2016 (Registration No: 3,756).

We are committed to providing immediate and long-term programmes to the most vulnerable communities without regard to creed, race, gender or ethnicity. We empower people to participate fully in matters of peace and developmental projects at both national and grassroots levels.

Our local presence spans Ayod, Bor South, Canal/Pigi, Akobo West, Uror, Fangak (Jonglei State), Koch & Rubkona (Unity State), and Malakal (Upper Nile State).

Learn More About Us

Goal

To enhance access to equitable development services focusing on underserved children and communities, improving access to Education, basic livelihoods and building resilient societies.

Vision

Promote unity of the communities, engagement and facilitating peaceful negotiations to attain developmental and livelihood initiatives through forums that transform communities and civic engagement in South Sudan.

Core Values

People are at the center of development. Transparency & accountability drive our actions. Sustainability is built into every intervention. A holistic approach develops the whole person: healthy body, mind, and spirit.

Our Approach

Collaborative, evidence-based, and culturally sensitive methodologies. We engage all actors through social and economic processes that enable positive change in attitudes toward war and create a paradigm shift in communities.

Our Programs

Thematic Focus Areas

We implement comprehensive programmes across seven core thematic areas, addressing the root causes of vulnerability and empowering communities from within.

Peacebuilding & Conflict Mitigation

Facilitating dialogue, reconciliation, and conflict resolution to foster lasting harmony among communities across South Sudan.

Learn More

General Protection (Child Protection & GBV)

Safeguarding vulnerable children and women through prevention, response, and advocacy against violence, abuse, and exploitation.

Learn More

Equitable Education Development

Increasing enrolment and retention of school-going children (5–18 years) with emphasis on gender equity, inclusiveness, and combating violence in schools.

Learn More

Food Security & Livelihood (FSL)

Promoting sustainable livelihoods, economic empowerment, climate-smart agriculture, and improving food security among vulnerable households.

Learn More

Health, WASH & Shelter

Improving health and nutrition status, providing access to safe water, sanitation, hygiene, and shelter for vulnerable populations.

Learn More

Women & Youth Empowerment

Championing gender equality, supporting women-led economic initiatives, and creating vocational opportunities for the next generation of leaders.

Learn More

Disaster Risk Reduction & Climate Change

Equipping communities with early warning systems, resilience strategies, and climate justice advocacy to mitigate the impacts of climate shocks.
