Who We Are

Building Stronger Communities Through Peace and Development

PEACE AND DEVELOPMENT ORGANIZATION (PDO) is a National Non-Governmental Organization (NNGO) and non-profit making organization founded in South Sudan since 2020. The Relief & Rehabilitation Commission (RRC) under Chapter 3, Section 10 of the NGOs Act, 2016 legally registers it in 2021. Our main focus areas include Peacebuilding, Protection, Education, Health/Nutrition, WASH/NFI, Food Security and Livelihood, Disaster Risks Reduction & Climate Change Management.

We are committed to provide immediate and long-term programmes to most vulnerable communities without regard to creed, race, gender or ethnicity, we empower people to participate fully in matter of peace and developmental projects on behalf of people both national and grassroots Forums. Our local presence covers Ayod, Bor South, Canal/Pigi, Akobo West, Uror, Fangak Counties (Jonglei State), Koch & Rubkona counties (Unity State) & Malakal county (Upper Nile State).

PDO is in Partnership with South Sudan NGO Forum, Health, WASH, FSL, Education, Protection clusters including member of South Sudan Civil Society Networks to promote meaningful Citizen participation in Peace Building and Climate justice in South Sudan.

Our Foundation

Goal, Vision & Core Values

Our Goal

To enhance access to equitable development services focusing on undeserved children and communities, improving access to Education, basic livelihoods and building resilience societies.

Vision

Promote unity of the communities, engagement and facilitating peaceful negotiations to attain developmental and livelihood initiatives through forums that transform communities and civic engagement in South Sudan.

Core Values

  • People are at the very center of development.
  • Transparency and accountability drive our actions.
  • Sustainability is built into every intervention.
  • Holistic approach: healthy body, healthy mind, healthy spirit.
Our Approach

Strategic Objectives

Increase holistic development services that strengthen resilience and reduce poverty, illiteracy & injustice.

Promote unity and peaceful co‑existence among all South Sudanese.

Encourage infrastructure development in programme areas (WASH, education, agriculture, health, etc.).

Engage all actors through social and economic processes that create paradigm shifts away from conflict.

Collaborate with partners through participatory, gender and poverty‑sensitive integrated development.

Provide practical tools for peaceful living, conflict resolution & violence prevention.

Facilitate social capital for poverty reduction by mobilizing communities towards sustainable development.

Undertake emergency items distribution, child protection, education and relief & rehabilitation projects.

Monitor communities and encourage individual contributions to peace through annual recognition & awards.

Programme Details

Main Activities & Approaches

Health & Nutrition

Improving health and nutrition status of vulnerable women, children, elderly people and all women of reproductive age through community-based sensitization, behavior change, health system strengthening and advocacy.

Equitable Education Development

Targeting girl and child focused educational inclusion, increasing enrolment and retention of school-going children (5-18 years) with emphasis on gender equity, inclusiveness, psychosocial training, and combating violence in schools.

Food Security & Livelihood

Implementing FSL programmes according to FSL Cluster commitments, promoting sustainable livelihoods, climate-smart agriculture, farmer field schools, community seed revolving system, and rural savings and lending schemes.

Climate Adaptation & Resilience

Climate change policy making and legislation to strengthen climate justice mainstreaming in budgeting and sensitive development programming and private sector in climate resilience investment and corporate governance.

Peace, Good Governance & Development

PDO participates in policy and legislative reforms, constitutional making process, youth voice in nation building, climate justice governance and mitigation, and advocacy on ratification of the Paris Agreement. Strengthening effective, citizen participation and inclusion in governance and responsive management of government resources.

Programmatic & Financial Capacity

Programme Operations

PDO has sound Programme Operations Policy (POP) Manual providing guidance in strategic planning, programme preparation and implementation, and accountability.

Financial Sustainability

Financial processes follow a Financial Policy Manual (FPM), based on Generally Acceptable Accounting Principles (GAAPs), ensuring transparency and efficient grant management.

Key Staff & Personnel

11 staff in Juba Head Office, plus 20 focal persons (volunteers) in Upper Nile, Jonglei & Unity states. All staff are trained in project design, financial management, peace & conflict mitigation, M&E, and proposal development. Current main donors: Board of Directors & Samaritan's Purse.

How We Work

Our Process

Every PDO intervention follows a structured, participatory cycle that puts communities at the heart of change.

1

Community Assessment

We begin by listening. Participatory assessments ensure interventions are locally owned and tailored to real needs.

2

Participatory Design

Communities co‑create project plans. Gender justice and disability inclusion are mainstreamed from the start.

3

Implementation

Trained staff and community volunteers deliver quality programmes using our Programme Operations Policy.

4

Monitoring & Learning

Continuous M&E and community feedback loops allow us to adapt, learn, and improve impact.
